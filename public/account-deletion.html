Delete Your Sucker! Account

Account deletion removes your Sucker! account and personal account data that is not required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or service integrity.

Start Deletion

Sign in to Sucker!, open Profile, choose Delete Account, and confirm Permanent Delete Account. The option is available directly inside the app and does not require contacting support.

What Happens Next

Deletion is immediate. Your active and completed games, invites, profile data, profile photos, notification tokens, and sign-in account are permanently removed. Shared games also disappear from your opponents' history.
