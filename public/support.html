Sucker! Support

Need help with Sucker! Battle Dice? Send us a message and include the device you use and what happened.

Email Support

Sign-in Help

Enter your email address and choose Send Code. Check the same address for the sign-in code, including its spam or junk folder. If you already have a password, choose Sign in with a password.

Multiplayer Help

Multiplayer games are asynchronous. After you finish a turn, the other player can take theirs whenever they are ready. Make sure the app is online if a game or invite does not update.

Account and Privacy

You can delete your account from Profile in the app. Learn more on the Account Deletion page or read the Privacy Policy.
